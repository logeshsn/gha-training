Core Guardrails

These are the rules that keep the whole thing from degenerating.
  • IDFC is the source of truth. If money is not effectively available there, you do not spend it on credit.
  • Autopay stays ON for all cards with full amount due.
  • Pay manually on 1st–3rd via HDFC BillPay only as an optimization layer.
  • IDFC buffer floor: ₹15,000 minimum. If it breaks, spending freezes.
  • Axis buffer floor: leave a few thousand there so EMIs and automated pulls never bounce.

Do / Don’t

The boring rules that actually matter.
  • Do: Review all cards on the 22nd.
  • Do: Pay all cards by the 3rd even if due date is later.
  • Do: Keep HDFC BillPay as bonus, not as your only defense.
  • Don’t: Spend based on available credit limit.
  • Don’t: Split payments from multiple accounts.
  • Don’t: Add more cards into active rotation for tiny rewards.

Autopay Logic

Your final payments architecture.
  • Primary: Manual HDFC BillPay on 1st–3rd to extract whatever cashback exists.
  • Fallback: All cards keep full-amount autopay active from IDFC.
  • Reason: Optimization without removing the safety net. Humans love acting invincible until the baby, work, and life all happen in one week.
  • Verification: After manual payment, check that total due becomes zero or near zero in the issuing app.
